✨ What Is SnapFade?

SnapFade is a temporary screenshot utility for Android. Instead of saving every screenshot as a permanent file in your gallery, SnapFade focuses on the clipboard. You take a screenshot, and the image goes to the clipboard so you can paste it into a chat, note, document, or form. The screenshot is not meant to become another file you have to manage later.

That simple difference can save a lot of space over time. Think about all the screenshots you take just to share a quick detail, confirm an address, send a code, or show someone a small piece of information. Most of those images are useful for seconds, not months. SnapFade is designed around that short-lived use case.

🔒 Why Clipboard-Only Screenshots?

Most screenshot tools assume every capture should be saved. SnapFade starts from a different idea: many screenshots are temporary by nature. You may only need the image long enough to paste it into another app. Once it has been pasted, the screenshot has done its job.

A clipboard-first approach keeps your storage cleaner. It also makes your screenshot routine faster because you are not opening the gallery, finding the latest image, and deciding whether to delete it. You take the screenshot, paste it, and continue with your task.

For average users, the benefit is easy to understand: fewer useless screenshots, less cleanup, and more free space for photos, videos, and apps you actually care about.

That is the point of the app. SnapFade is built around a clipboard-only workflow, so the capture is meant for pasting rather than for permanent storage. Paste it into the app where you need it while it is still on your clipboard.
